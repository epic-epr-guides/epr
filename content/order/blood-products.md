# Blood Products

To order blood products (e.g. Packed Red Blood Cells (PRBCs), Fresh Frozen Plasma (FFP), or Platelets):

1. Search for **'Adult General Blood Administration'** in **Orders** and open the order set

2. Patients need 2 sample **'Group and Screen'** samples
   - 1 historic sample on the system
   - 1 valid sample within the last 7 days
   - check for previous samples within the **Results Review** tab and search **'Blood Group'**
   - Order new G&S if no valid sample

3. You can prescribe adjunct medication during transfusions within this order panel e.g. furosemide to reduce risk of TACO

4. Select the relevant blood product
   - The **'Prepare'** Order sends a request to blood bank to prepare the rquired number of units
   - THe **'Transfusion'** Order allows ward staff to admininster the blood products

You can request multiple units using the Prepare order if you anticipate the patient will require more than one unit.

You can initially prescribe a single Transfusion order and add further transfusion orders later if additional units are required.
