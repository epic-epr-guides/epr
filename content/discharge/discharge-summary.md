# Discharge Summary

A useful SmartPhrase for completing discharge summaries is:

`.edlgimopu`

If you keep the patient's Hospital Course up to date throughout their admission, this SmartPhrase will automatically pull the information from that note and pre-populate the Hospital Course section of the discharge summary.

Using this SmartPhrase can significantly reduce the time required to complete discharge summaries while ensuring consistency and accuracy.

> **Tip:** Regularly updating the Hospital Course during the admission makes discharge summaries quicker to complete and helps ensure important clinical information is not missed.
