# SmartPhrases

SmartPharses are text shortcuts that automatically populate a note with a template. There are many SmartPhrases within the system and you can create your own.

![](./media/smartphrases.mp4)

Useful SmartPhrases that I use often include:

- `.FALLINPATIENTMEDICALREVIEW`
- `.EDLGIMOPU`
- `.2222`
- `.OPUMDM`
- `.CrCl`

There are many more and you can create your own or take from other people.

Select 'My Tools' at the top and 'My Smartphrases'.

Here you can see which smartphrases you have access to;

Create new smart phrases and search for exisiting smartphrases

You can search for other users and add their smartphrases to your list.
