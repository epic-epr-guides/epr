# Welcome

This site holds the step-by-step support guides for the Electronic Patient Record (EPR) system.

Every guide you see here is a file written by the IT support team. Nothing on this site is
generated automatically, so if a guide says something, a person wrote it.

## How this site is organised

Guides are grouped into categories. The menu button in the top bar opens the list of
categories; tap a category to see the guides inside it, then tap a guide to read it.

On a tablet or a computer the same list appears as a permanent menu down the left-hand side.

## What is here at the moment

Only these introductory pages. The EPR guides themselves are added by the IT support team —
see [Adding a Guide](../for-administrators/adding-a-guide.md) if that is your job.

## If something looks wrong

If a guide is out of date, contradicts local policy, or will not open, contact the IT support
team. Do not follow a clinical instruction from this site if it conflicts with your
department's current procedure.
