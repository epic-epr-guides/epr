# Finding a Guide

## On a phone

1. Tap the **Menu** button at the top left of the screen.
2. The list of categories slides in from the left.
3. Tap a category to open it. Tap it again to close it.
4. Tap the guide you want. The menu closes on its own and the guide opens.

To go back, use the trail of links just under the top bar, or your phone's back button.

## On a tablet or computer

The category list is always visible down the left-hand side. Click a category to open it,
then click a guide.

## Using a keyboard

- **Tab** moves between links and buttons.
- **Enter** or **Space** opens the highlighted item.
- **Escape** closes the menu on a phone.

## Sharing a guide

The address in your browser changes as you move around, and it points at the exact guide you
are reading. Copy it and send it to a colleague and they will land on the same page.

## There is no search

This site has no search box yet. Browse by category instead. If you cannot find something you
are certain should be here, contact the IT support team.
