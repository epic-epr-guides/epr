# Making Referrals

Referrals to some specialties can be made directly through EPIC **Orders**.

1. Select **Add Orders**.
2. Search for **Inpatient Consult to...** followed by the relevant specialty.
3. Complete the required referral information, ensuring all relevant clinical details are included.

> **Please note:** Submitting the referral order does not always notify the specialty team. You may also need to contact the team directly to ensure the referral is received. This can usually be done by:

- Sending a **SmartPage**, or
- Contacting the team via the hospital switchboard.
