# Outpatient Referrals

Outpatient referrals can be made from either the **Orders** tab or while completing the patient's discharge paperwork.

## Creating an Outpatient Referral

1. Navigate to either:
   - **Orders** and select **Outpatient**, or
   - **Discharge Orders** while completing the patient's TTOs.
2. Search for **Outpatient Referral to...** followed by the relevant specialty or service.
3. Select the appropriate referral.
4. Complete all required referral information, including the relevant clinical details.
5. **Sign** and submit the referral.

> **Please note:** Some outpatient services require additional steps after the referral has been submitted, e.g. Hospital at Home referrals also require you to telephone the service to discuss the referral and confirm acceptance.
