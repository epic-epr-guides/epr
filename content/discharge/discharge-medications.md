# Discharge Medications (TTOs)

To complete a patient's discharge medications (TTOs):

1. Navigate to the **Discharge** tab.
2. Select **Discharge Orders**.
3. Click **Review Orders for Discharge**.

The medication list is divided into:

**Home Medications** – Medications the patient was taking before admission.

**Hospital Orders** – Medications prescribed during the admission.

Use the action icons to decide what should happen to each medication on discharge:

- ▶ **Resume** – Continue the medication on discharge.
- ⏸ **Hold** – Temporarily withhold the medication.
- ✏ **Modify** – Change the dose, route, frequency, or other prescription details.
- ✖ **Stop** – Discontinue the medication.
- ➕ **Add** – Prescribe a hospital order for discharge

Please note: In most cases, the majority of the patient's home medications will be resumed unless there is a clinical reason to stop or modify them.

## Adding New Medications

To prescribe a new medication for discharge:

1. Search within the discharge orders screen to find the required medication.
2. Complete the prescription details and save the order.

Carefully review the final discharge medication list to ensure all intended medications have been continued, stopped, modified, or added before completing the TTO.
