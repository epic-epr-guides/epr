# Ordering Imaging

To request imaging:

1. Select **Add Orders**.
2. Search for the required imaging investigation.
3. Complete the request with the relevant clinical information and **sign** the order.

## X-rays and Ultrasounds

Most X-ray and ultrasound requests do not require radiologist vetting before they can be performed.

After submitting the request:

Contact the relevant imaging department to arrange the scan if it has not already been scheduled.

**St Thomas' Hospital**

- In-hours X-ray: Ext. 85484
- Out-of-hours X-ray: Ext. 85469
- Ultrasound: Ext. 85543 or 89868

**Guy's Hospital**

- In-hours X-ray: Ext. 85512

## CT and MRI

CT and MRI requests require radiologist vetting before they can be scheduled, although some requests may already have be vetted shortly after requesting

### Checking the Vetting Status

1. After submitting the request, open **Chart Review**.
2. Select the **Imaging** tab.
3. Review the **Protocol** status of the requested scan.

- **Completed** – The scan has been vetted and can be scheduled.
- **Needed** – The request is awaiting radiologist review and must be vetted before scheduling.

### Contacting the Radiologist

If the protocol status is **Needed**, contact the radiologist:

- In-hours Radiologist: Ext. 56525 or 56373
- Out-of-hours Radiologist: Contact via SmartPage or the hospital switchboard.

> **Important:** Out of hours, radiologists will generally only vet scans that are clinically urgent. Examples include:

CT Head (CTH) for suspected intracranial haemorrhage.

MRI Spine for suspected metastatic spinal cord compression (MSCC).

Other time-critical imaging where immediate results are required to guide patient management.
