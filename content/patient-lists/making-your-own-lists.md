# Making your own Patient List

To create your own patient list, select **'Edit List'** on the top left and **'Create My List'**. You can title your list and select custom columns using the search box.

Within the **Criteria** section you can **'Add teams'** which will automatically add patient's assigned to that team to your list.

Within the **Advanced** section your can add other users making this a 'Shared Patient List'
